# voidvision application demonstration - 1 minute video script

## video overview
**duration**: 60 seconds  
**format**: application demonstration with two presenters  
**purpose**: showcase voidvision's real-time safety equipment detection capabilities  

## characters
- **presenter 1 (sumedha)**: space station safety officer - demonstrates the application
- **presenter 2 (vartika)**: mission control operator - provides context and commentary

---

## script breakdown

### scene 1: introduction (0-10 seconds)
**[camera shows both presenters in space station control room setting]**

**sumedha**: "welcome to voidvision, our ai-powered space station safety monitor."

**vartika**: "this system detects critical safety equipment in real-time to ensure astronaut safety."

**[camera zooms to computer screen showing voidvision interface]**

---

### scene 2: application launch (10-20 seconds)
**[sumedha clicks to launch streamlit app]**

**sumedha**: "let me show you how it works. first, we launch the safety monitor application."

**[screen shows voidvision interface loading]**

**vartika**: "the system uses yolov8 ai to detect seven types of critical safety equipment."

**[interface fully loaded, showing space-themed dashboard]**

---

### scene 3: real-time detection demo (20-45 seconds)
**[sumedha uploads a test image of space station interior]**

**sumedha**: "now i'll upload an image of our space station module."

**[image appears on screen with detection results showing]**

**vartika**: "watch as the ai instantly identifies safety equipment with bounding boxes."

**[camera shows detection results highlighting oxygen tank, fire extinguisher, first aid box]**

**sumedha**: "here we can see oxygen tank, fire extinguisher, and first aid box detected with high confidence."

**[sumedha adjusts confidence slider]**

**sumedha**: "we can adjust the confidence threshold to fine-tune detection sensitivity."

**[vartika points to safety score display]**

**vartika**: "the system calculates an overall safety score and alerts us to any missing equipment."

---

### scene 4: alert demonstration (45-55 seconds)
**[sumedha uploads image with missing fire extinguisher]**

**sumedha**: "let me show you what happens when critical equipment is missing."

**[red alert appears on screen showing "fire extinguisher missing"]**

**vartika**: "immediate alerts ensure we never miss critical safety equipment."

**[sumedha clicks export button]**

**sumedha**: "all results can be exported for safety reports and compliance tracking."

---

### scene 5: conclusion (55-60 seconds)
**[camera pulls back to show both presenters]**

**vartika**: "voidvision ensures astronaut safety through continuous ai monitoring."

**sumedha**: "deployed and ready for space station missions."

**[screen shows voidvision logo with tagline]**

**both**: "ensuring astronaut safety through ai-powered equipment monitoring."

---
